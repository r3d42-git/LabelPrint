# LabelPrint icon

Created with the built-in image generation tool. The final PNG was resized proportionally
to 1024 × 1024 for macOS packaging. Transparency was retained.

## Initial prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Asset: production macOS application icon for LabelPrint, a simple local PDF cropping and thermal-label printing utility. Create exactly one polished square 1024 x 1024 icon, no mockup, no text, no letters, no numbers, no external objects. A deep forest-green rounded macOS squircle tile, color family #156b57, centered with standard generous transparent outer margins, all corners fully visible. On the tile a large warm white portrait adhesive shipping label, very gently tilted clockwise, with slightly rounded corners and one small subtly peeled bottom corner. On the white label a bold dark charcoal barcode composed of a handful of crisp varying-width parallel vertical bars, plus two short muted green horizontal address lines above it. The barcode is a symbolic icon, not functional code. Very clear simple silhouette, restrained softly dimensional material and gentle shadow, premium calm utility aesthetic, high contrast, readable at 32 pixels. Large uncomplicated shapes, no microdetails, no printer hardware, no QR pattern, no gradients outside the green tile. Background outside the squircle truly transparent; never a white or checkered square background.

## Cleanup prompt

Precise cleanup of this finished LabelPrint macOS icon. Preserve the exact composition, proportions, deep green rounded-square tile, off-white label, barcode and folded corner. Change only the silhouette edge and exterior: remove every stray green speck, isolated pixel or noise outside the rounded-square tile, especially along the top margin. Make the outer contour smooth, clean and professionally antialiased, with a completely transparent empty exterior and even padding. Do not add any elements, text, framing or checkerboard. Deliver a square app icon asset, ideally 1024 x 1024.
